Openorona brings Fanorona, the traditional strategy board game from Madagascar, to Android.

• Three board variants: Fanorona-Telo (3×3), Fanorona-Dimy (5×5), and Fanorona-Tsivy (9×5)
• Computer opponent with five difficulty levels
• Pass and play with optional per-player timers
• Automatic saving and continue
• Move undo, board themes, and optional vibration

No ads or account required. Games are saved on your device.
